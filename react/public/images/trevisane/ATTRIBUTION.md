# Trevisane (Venetian pattern) card skin

These 40 card images were cropped from four public-domain scans of 18th-century
Venetian-pattern playing cards (colored woodcuts, the historical ancestor of the
modern Trevisane/Trevigiane deck) held by the Museo Civico Correr, Venice
(Cl. XXX n. 0084), sourced from Wikimedia Commons:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_veneziane_-_denari_-_Museo_Correr_-_Cl._XXX_n._0084.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_veneziane_-_coppe_-_Museo_Correr_-_Cl._XXX_n._0084.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_veneziane_-_spade_-_Museo_Correr_-_Cl._XXX_n._0084.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carte_veneziane_-_bastoni_-_Museo_Correr_-_Cl._XXX_n._0084.jpg

License: public domain (published before 1931; faithful reproductions of
two-dimensional public-domain works are themselves public domain).

Naming follows the app convention `{rank}{suit}.png` — suits b/c/d/s =
bastoni/coppe/denari/spade; ranks 1–7 are numerals, 8 = fante, 9 = cavallo, 10 = re.
